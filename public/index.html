Rules, without the noise

Canonical rules
01

Everyone starts equal

Each DID receives 10,000 POLF once. POLF is contest accounting, not a wallet token.

02

A call needs two signatures

A long only exists when another registered DID accepts the short side, or vice versa.

03

Five-minute sweeps

Nothing is final until the referee lists the trade as settled. Prices must stay inside the posted ±5% range.

04

No leverage

The full trade value is tied up. Each side pays 1%, with the official clawback rule applied when larger.

05

One final NVDA price

Trading locks 4 October at 09:00 UTC. The last Hyperliquid xyz:NVDA trade before 10:00 UTC settles every open position.

06

Top three win

The three highest scores share 1,000,000 FLOP after mainnet, under the official claim rules.
