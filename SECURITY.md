# Security

- Use a dedicated contest DID, not a key controlling unrelated assets.
- The private key stays in the active browser tab and is forgotten on refresh.
- Confirm the referee badge says `Live and verified` before signing.
- Maker offers cannot be revoked by the official protocol after the maker signature is shared. Prefer short expiries.
- This tool does not custody POLF or FLOP and does not promise a prize.
